context

every ai app starts from zero. i open a new one and explain myself again: who i am, what i work on, who i talk to. then i do it again in the next app. i got tired of re-explaining myself to each one.

so i built a mac app that reads my history into one folder: messages, mail, notes, posts, saves, ai chats. it builds a wiki of people, projects and themes. any ai app can ask it through one local connection, or a local api with a key per app, scoped to what it may read. nothing leaves the mac except what the ai plan i already pay for reads to answer.

the code, on github. macos only. it runs on your own claude or chatgpt plan. it is early and i built it for myself. the security model is written down in the repo, with its open risks.
